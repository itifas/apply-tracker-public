# Privacy

Last updated 23 November 2026. The same notice is shown in the app at `/privacy`.

Apply Tracker is a personal project by Iakovos Tifas. The web demo has no accounts, no advertising and no tracking.

## What the app stores

Everything you enter (companies, roles, contacts, notes, dates and follow-ups) is saved only on your device:

- **Web demo:** in your browser's local storage. It never reaches a server. Clearing your browser data or choosing "Reset sample data" deletes it.
- **Desktop app:** in the app's storage folder on your computer. Uninstalling the app and removing that folder deletes it.

Because this data is never received by the author, it can't be seen, shared or recovered. Use Export in Settings to keep a copy.

## Hosting

The web demo is served by Cloudflare. To deliver pages and protect the site, Cloudflare processes technical request data such as IP address, browser type and the pages requested, and keeps request logs for a limited time. The app doesn't set cookies of its own. See [Cloudflare's privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).

## Desktop updates

On start, the desktop app asks GitHub whether a newer version exists. GitHub sees your IP address as part of that request. No tracker data is sent.

## GDPR

If you're in the European Economic Area or the UK, you have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your personal data, and to complain to your data protection authority.

- For the web demo, the only personal data processed is the request data above, on the basis of legitimate interest in running a secure website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
- Tracker data stays under your sole control and can be deleted at any time as described above.
- If you run your own server copy of Apply Tracker (the PostgreSQL version), you are the controller of the data it stores and need your own privacy notice.

## Planned online version

We plan to offer an optional online version with an account, so your tracker can be stored in our database and used on several devices. Nothing in this section happens yet. If it launches:

- **Optional.** The web demo and the desktop app keep working without an account, with data only on your device.
- **You agree first.** Before anything is uploaded you'll see an updated version of this notice and be asked to agree. Nothing already on your device is uploaded unless you choose to.
- **Analysis only with permission.** If you allow it, data in your account may be analysed to improve Apply Tracker and to publish general insights about job searching (for example typical response times, or how many applications lead to interviews). Analysis uses anonymised, combined data; results won't identify you or anyone in your tracker. Contact details of recruiters and other people you add are left out.
- **No selling, no advertising.** Personal data won't be sold or used for ads.
- **Your control.** You can download your data, withdraw permission for analysis, and delete your account at any time.

Under the GDPR, storing your account data would rely on the contract to provide the service (Art. 6(1)(b)), and analysis on your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)).

Apply Tracker is not directed at children under 16.
